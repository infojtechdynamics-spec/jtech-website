Product overview

J-Tech's Escrow Application provides a controlled framework for holding funds until agreed conditions are met. It helps buyers, sellers, SACCOs, enterprises, and service providers complete transactions with clear verification, audit trails, and secure approvals.

Overview

A reliable layer of digital trust

Our Escrow Application acts as a digital trust infrastructure, serving as a secure neutral layer in any exchange. It ensures that financial commitments and service delivery obligations are perfectly synchronized, moving funds only when all conditions are met.

Why It Matters

In an increasingly decentralized global economy, trust is the scarcest resource. Our solution bridges the 'trust gap' by drastically reducing transaction uncertainty—virtually eliminating fraud and failed deliveries. We provide the ironclad guarantee needed for enterprises to engage in complex transactions with total confidence.

How it works

The Escrow Lifecycle

A synchronized 6-step flow designed for absolute transparency and security.

01

Initiation

Buyer initiates transaction and defines conditions.

02

Secure Hold

Funds are held in a secure multi-sig vault.

03

Delivery

Seller delivers product or provides service.

04

Validation

Buyer confirms delivery or completion criteria.

05

Release

Funds are automatically released to the seller.

06

Resolution

Dispute resolution workflows for any discrepancies.
